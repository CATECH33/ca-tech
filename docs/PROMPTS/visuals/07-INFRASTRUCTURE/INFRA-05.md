# INFRA-05 — Deployment Pipeline

## OBJECTIVE

Représenter le déploiement continu d'une application : quelque chose qui part "en production".
Mouvement, progression, aboutissement. Pas de bouton "Deploy" ni d'interface Vercel.

## USE CASE

- Feature "Déploiement automatisé"
- Section CI/CD / DevOps
- Process CA-TECH: de la construction au lancement

## PROMPT

```
rocket or projectile on launch rail in dark environment, extreme close-up of rear section,
cold blue engine glow or rail guidance system, dark technical housing,
forward motion blur suggesting imminent departure,
deep navy environment, industrial precision,
no consumer aerospace, purely technical and abstract,
dramatic angle, slight motion blur,
cinematic product photography, precision engineering aesthetic,
shot on Sony A7R V, 85mm, f/2.8, slow shutter,
--ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --q 2
--no NASA branding, colorful rocket, space stars background, cartoon, purple, neon
```

## VARIANTE — Rail / Track

```
close-up of precision machined rail with something in guided motion,
cold blue guidance light indicators along the rail,
deep perspective receding, dark industrial environment,
motion blur in the direction of travel,
purely abstract engineering,
--ar 3:2 --style raw --v 7 --q 2
```

## NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS

- Pas de branding spatial (NASA, SpaceX...)
- Pas de couleurs vives
- Pas de contexte "gaming" ou "sci-fi Hollywood"
- L'esthétique doit rester proche de l'ingénierie de précision réelle

## ASPECT RATIO

`--ar 3:2` — 1200×800 px

## CAMERA / LENS

Sony A7R V — 85mm — f/2.8 — slight motion blur

## LIGHTING

Seule source : glow propulsif ou lumières de guidage (cold blue).
